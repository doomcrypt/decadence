The Deck

Remove all royal cards, tens, and jokers from a standard pack. The remainder is a Decadence pack of thirty-six cards (1 through 9, four suits).

The Cross

Five cards are dealt face up (Set-1) in the Atlantean Cross: Centre (Memories & Dreams), West (Destructive), East (Creative), North (Far Future), South (Deep Past).

Pairing

Five cards are dealt face down (Set-2). Turn them over one at a time. Each Set-2 card may be paired with a Set-1 card if their values sum to ten. A pair scores the difference between the two cards (5+5=0, 9+1=8). A Set-2 card that cannot pair is discarded. An unpaired Set-1 card scores its raw value negatively.

Scoring

Round scores can be positive or negative. Positive scores accumulate to the Angelic Index. The Aeon ends on the first negative result. Negative scores (0 to −44) call a demon from the Pandemonium Matrix.
